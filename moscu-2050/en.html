What if the past you remember never existed?

Hypothetical scenario

“Moscow, 2050. In this imagined future, algorithms do not only shape the information we receive. They can also build images of a past that never happened.”

In this city the past is not told — it is generated. There is no single shared reality: what a person knows about the past depends on their access to technology.

Scenario · Earth 2050 predictions for Moscow

Our scenario is built on the 16 predictions for Moscow in 2050 on the Earth 2050 platform (Kaspersky). They are guesses by futurologists and users, not facts. These are the ones that shape our position:

    A mediated reality. You no longer need to be in a place to “live” it, or to learn the past first-hand: a screen, a headset or an algorithm decides.

    A fragile reality. Daily life depends on systems we neither understand nor control.

    The risks of that future already have real precedents. Tap a card to see an example.

            Project question

            If memory is a part of being human that we do not want to automate, how could graphic design give people back control over how their own past is told?

            1. You send the material and tell us where it comes from.
            2. A person — not an algorithm — reviews the source and context.
            3. It is only published with its verification status visible.
